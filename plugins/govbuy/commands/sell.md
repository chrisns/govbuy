The user is a vendor and wants to sell to the UK public sector: **$ARGUMENTS**

72=IT, 48=software, 35=security, 45=construction, 85=health, 90=environmental, …). Then build their go-to-market:

- **Frameworks & live DPS you can JOIN** now (a DPS stays open to new suppliers throughout its life).

- **Live opportunities + the forward pipeline** (planned procurements 6–18 months out) to prepare bids for.
- **Incumbents to displace** — who holds the spend and when their contracts END (your window).
- **Resellers / thin-primes** who could carry you in faster than direct appointment.

sub-threshold local notices). Verify each opportunity on its `official_url`. Every figure and name comes verbatim
from the tool result.
